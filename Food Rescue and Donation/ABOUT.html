About FoodRescue

A student-designed platform connecting surplus food with NGOs to reduce waste and support local communities.

Our Vision

We envision clean, localized cities where edible food is shared instead of wasted. FoodRescue creates a simple and reliable connection between food donors and NGOs so that surplus food can reach people who need it.

How We Make A Difference

Reduce Food Waste

Keeping safe and edible food away from landfills by encouraging timely donation and redistribution.

Feed Communities

Helping NGOs receive surplus food and distribute nutritious meals to communities in need.

Build Partnerships

Creating dependable connections between restaurants, institutions, event organizers and NGOs.

The Student Initiative Behind the Code

FoodRescue started as a student social-impact engineering project focused on solving the everyday problem of food waste. We designed a simple digital platform that helps donors connect with NGOs that can collect and distribute surplus food.

The idea is simple: instead of throwing away extra food from restaurants, hostels, gurudwaras, universities and events, safe surplus food can be redirected to people who need it.
