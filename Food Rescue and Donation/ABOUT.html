About FoodRescue

A student-designed initiative dedicated to local sustainability and immediate food equity.

Our Vision

We envision clean, localized cities where food surplus is never treated as trash. By creating an organized, reliable connection between donors and NGOs, we hope to normalize zero-waste catering, and ensure food resources serve the community first.

How We Make A Difference

Reduce Food Waste

Keeping edible food out of trash by choosing preservation and donation.

Feed Communities

Channeling nutrition directly to shelters, communities and people in need.

Build Partnerships

Constructing reliable connections between local organizations and NGOs.

The Student Initiative Behind the Code

FoodRescue began as a student social-impact engineering project. We designed this simple platform to connect people and organizations with extra food to NGOs that can distribute it.

The main idea is simple. Instead of throwing away leftover food from universities, gurudwaras, restaurants and events, we can give it to someone who needs it.
