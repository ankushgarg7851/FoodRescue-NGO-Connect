OUR PURPOSE

Our Vision

At FoodRescue, our vision is to create a world where good food does not go to waste while people go hungry. We connect restaurants, canteens, hostels, event organizers, NGOs, and volunteers through one platform to make food redistribution easier and more effective.

OUR STORY

A Student Initiative Behind the Code

FoodRescue is a student-driven project built around a simple idea: technology can help solve real-world problems.

By connecting food donors, NGOs, and volunteers, we aim to make surplus food recovery easier, more accessible, and more organized.

Our goal is to use technology for social good and encourage people to take practical steps toward reducing food waste.
